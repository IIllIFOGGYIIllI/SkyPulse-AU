Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

SkyPulse AU is an independent, non-commercial website and installable Progressive Web App. We do not ask you to create an account.

Information stored on your device

Location choice, chosen alert radius, theme and browser-only notification preference are stored in your browser's local storage. You can remove them by clearing site data for SkyPulse AU in your browser settings.

Location permissions

If you choose “Use my location”, your browser requests permission to access an approximate or precise device location according to your browser and device settings. The location coordinates are used to request weather forecasts and select map and incident views. They remain in your browser's saved preferences until cleared or replaced. We do not operate a user-location database in this release.

External services

When you visit SkyPulse AU, your browser makes requests to services including GitHub Pages (hosting), Open-Meteo (weather and location search), RainViewer (radar), OpenStreetMap tile infrastructure (maps), Google Fonts, and CDN providers used for the map library. Those providers may receive your IP address, requested URLs and technical information according to their own privacy policies. Weather and map requests necessarily include your selected geographic coordinates or map area.

Notifications

Any browser notification permission is controlled by your browser. This release supports only new-incident notifications while the app is open, where supported. There are no background push subscriptions, notification server, or user accounts in this release.

Analytics, advertising and sale of data

We have not added advertising, tracking analytics or a personal-data sale mechanism. Third-party infrastructure may keep standard operational logs.

Security and children

Only use the official HTTPS site. Do not enter sensitive personal information into location search. The public site is intended for general audiences and is not designed to collect children's personal information.

Changes and contact

This policy will be revised when features or data flows materially change. Project issues may be submitted through the GitHub issue tracker; please do not post personal or sensitive information publicly.
