Terms of Use & Emergency Disclaimer

Last updated: 15 November 2026

EMERGENCY: call Triple Zero (000) in Australia if life or property is in immediate danger. SkyPulse AU is not an official warning authority, emergency communications provider or government service. Never rely solely on this app to make safety decisions.

What the service provides

SkyPulse AU aggregates non-commercial weather forecasts, historical radar imagery, incident locations and links to official NSW/Australian information. Information can be incorrect, incomplete, unavailable, geographically inaccurate or delayed. Not every weather or incident layer covers every part of Australia.

Warnings and safety

NSW RFS and Bureau of Meteorology source information may be delayed by the original provider and by our scheduled publication process. The app displays retrieval times, not a guarantee of issue time or live data freshness. NSW/ACT BOM warning titles are statewide, not necessarily applicable to your selected suburb. Incident distance estimates use mapped coordinates and do not determine whether a warning area affects you. A displayed absence of incidents is not proof of safety.

Check Hazards Near Me NSW, BOM warnings, NSW RFS, NSW SES HazardWatch, official instructions and emergency broadcasts directly.

Notification limitations

The included opt-in background push infrastructure requires an external Cloudflare Worker and does not provide guaranteed delivery, official emergency warning delivery or SMS. An RFS warning without a machine-readable polygon may not generate location-based push notifications. WMO-listed BOM publications are not automatically treated as current local warnings. Optional on-screen/browser notifications for newly discovered nearby mapped incidents function only while the app is open and supported by your browser. Timeliness is not guaranteed.
