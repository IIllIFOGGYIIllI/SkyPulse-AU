Data Sources & Attribution

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Every data source has its own coverage, update schedule, licence and reliability limitations. External links open the original publisher's site.

Forecasts and location search

Open-Meteo provides forecast, hourly and daily weather data and location geocoding for non-commercial use. Open-Meteo data is attributed under CC BY 4.0; forecasts are model-based estimates, not official BOM observations.

Radar

RainViewer provides historical rainfall radar frames via its publicly documented API for personal/educational/small-scale community use. Radar images are not guaranteed available, may be delayed, and are not forecasts. The free API has zoom and usage limits.

Map tiles

© OpenStreetMap contributors provides map geographic data; tiles are served by OSM infrastructure under the applicable tile usage policy.

NSW Rural Fire Service

NSW RFS Current Incidents GeoJSON is used to display NSW fire incident locations. The data is published through Data.NSW under Creative Commons Attribution; incident data is reportedly updated around every 30 minutes at source. SkyPulse AU has additional scheduled publication delay. All decisions should be based on NSW RFS guidance.

NSW RFS Fire Danger Ratings & Total Fire Bans

NSW RFS Fire Danger XML feed provides district fire danger ratings and Total Fire Ban indicators for today and tomorrow. Attribution: © NSW Rural Fire Service. Data.NSW lists the dataset under Creative Commons Attribution. The map from a location to its fire weather district may need manual selection. A feed retrieval timestamp is not an official issue time. Always consult NSW RFS current fire danger information before making safety decisions.

Official Weather Station Observations

BOM Cessnock Airport AWS publishes measured weather conditions such as temperature, wind, dew point, pressure and rainfall since 9 am. SkyPulse AU links to these observations and does not copy them into its dashboard. Open-Meteo current conditions are model-derived and should not be interpreted as Cessnock Airport measurements.

Satellite Imagery

The dashboard links to the Bureau of Meteorology Satellite Viewer. Any artwork shown in the SkyPulse satellite card is illustrative and is not actual or live satellite data.

Official RFS CAP Warnings & Warning Areas

NSW RFS warning feeds publish alerts in an EDXL/CAP structure. Where the alert contains valid CAP polygons, SkyPulse AU can display those areas. Some alerts have no usable polygons and cannot be automatically matched to your watch zone. Attribution: © State of New South Wales (NSW Rural Fire Service). For current information go to www.rfs.nsw.gov.au.

WMO-Indexed Bureau Publications

The World Meteorological Organization's register points to a public feed of Bureau-issued CAP publications. SkyPulse AU lists recent NSW/ACT-related title matches as supplementary references, not confirmed currently active warnings or location-filtered emergency notices. The official BOM warning service remains authoritative.

Weather warnings

Australian Bureau of Meteorology RSS is intended to provide statewide NSW/ACT warning titles with direct links to full official notices, but the current scheduled importer receives HTTP 403 and the data is labelled unavailable. SkyPulse AU does not claim that there are no active warnings when the feed cannot be accessed. This service is subject to BOM's personal and non-commercial RSS terms, and should never be used as the sole source during warning events.

NSW SES, official alerts, road incidents and news

The app links directly to NSW SES HazardWatch, Hazards Near Me NSW, Live Traffic NSW, ABC Newcastle and other publishers. NSW SES warning data is not reproduced because its direct feed needs separate authorisation; the website links to official sources instead.

Update status

Weather API fetches occur on demand in the browser. RFS and BOM snapshots are fetched by GitHub Actions on a best-effort scheduled basis. A snapshot timestamp indicates retrieval only. If updating fails, the app should mark the source unavailable or stale.
